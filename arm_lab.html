EMG arm lab

A simulated forearm: you move the fingers, muscles fire, the sensors around the arm pick it up. Train on one arm position, then move the arm or hide sensors: the model rebuilds the whole hand it sees (blue) and names the mode. Poses it does not know are called CUSTOM. The Train ±° slider widens training across arm rotations so the model keeps working when the arm moves. Simulated data, not a real arm.
